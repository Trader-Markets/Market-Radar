Entry, stop and target remain the backend-confirmed plan references. They are planned exit levels, not guaranteed fills.

Actual fills and position monitoring are in My Positions; this scanner list does not assert knowledge of a fill or tell customers to exit merely because an alert expired or data became unavailable.
