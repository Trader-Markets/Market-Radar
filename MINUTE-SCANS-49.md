# One-minute scanning and separate live-volume tab — build49

29 October 2026. Crypto → Setups now has a separate **Live volume & big move** tab. It has visible start/pause controls linked to the existing saved Early Rally continuous-scan switch. Only one setup section is visible at a time; switching tabs retains scan state. Streaming warnings remain event-driven with two-second status checks, not delayed until a minute boundary. Delta universe reconciliation now happens every minute; a list older than three minutes blocks new warnings.

Enabled Crypto market scanner checks are scheduled every60seconds while the browser is visible, including while another asset workspace is selected. A busy sweep continues without overlapping restart. Existing Auto scan checkbox remains the opt-in. Top20/Early Rally browser checks run every60seconds and retry failed scans on the next timer. Backend job reuse changed from five-minute to one-minute slots, so same5m candles no longer silently prevent a new minute scan. Local runner drains bounded batches continuously and schedules a fresh completed sweep after60seconds; start failures back off60seconds. Candle periods and confirmation gates stay unchanged, including hourly close plus follow-through for final Top20 alerts. OI/funding sampling still uses its original five-minute sampling buckets to avoid inventing baseline evidence.

Saved Crypto background watchlist now uses minute job slots and minute duplicate-run suppression. Local watchlist timer already ran every60seconds; its old hourly guard is removed. Saved Indian watchlist symbols are due after60seconds instead of an hour. Its local pump checks one due symbol at a time with a two-second scheduling tick, respecting server locks, saved enable state and pause/revision guards. Slow provider responses can delay a full8-symbol pass; requests are never overlapped by that runner.

Indian selected-instrument strategy page can repeat every60seconds after an initial successful manual check; a visible checkbox controls it. Invalidated symbol changes clear old scan data. Full NSE screener can repeat completed sweeps on a60second timer after Start/Resume, with a visible repeat checkbox. An unfinished sweep is not restarted every minute. Pause, navigation cleanup, failed preflight, authentication failure and persistent quota failures suppress repeats. Full NSE sweeps cannot be guaranteed to finish within60seconds; per-stock timestamps and bounded pacing are preserved.

No new global automated strategy backend is introduced. Global public widgets follow their provider's data/refresh cadence. Hosted closed-app scans still depend on their configured schedule; this patch does NOT provision an always-running hosted collector or change that cloud schedule. Local server/PC must remain running/awake for existing browser-independent features. Browser-only full-market scans require the page running and can be throttled/suspended by browsers. No data latency, fakeout elimination, message delivery or all-symbol completion time is guaranteed.

Existing paused settings, broker keys, risk settings, recipients and sharing are preserved. No test trades, positions or messages were placed/sent.
