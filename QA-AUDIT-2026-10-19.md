# Market Radar audit — 19 October 2026 (IST)

## Result

44 automated test files passed, zero failed. Worker build and JavaScript checks passed. This includes an actual isolated localhost HTTP server, alongside simulated exchange/UI tests. These results do not establish flawless live trading, browser rendering, or every market feed.

## New defects repaired

- Chart-review null/malformed responses and incomplete evidence rows could enter rendering and break retry controls. Invalid batches are rejected before being added to the analysis.
- Incomplete performance reports could replace the report and then fail during calendar rendering. The response is checked first; failed loads retain recovery controls.
- A same-instrument Indian chart refresh left old candles/results visible during the new request. The old chart and plan lines now clear before loading; invalid data is rejected and retry remains available.
- Local startup ran trade tracking and volume batches, but never scheduled the saved hourly background watchlist. A non-overlapping timer now runs only explicitly saved enabled settings, skips completed hourly slots, respects pauses, and stops with the server. Existing server delivery preferences control alerts. Unsaved defaults do not start scanning.
- Saint selections could remain selected after changing audio source even though their player had been removed. The selection now resets so the same recording can be selected again.
