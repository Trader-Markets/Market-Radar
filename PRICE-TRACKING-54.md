# Independent price tracking for volume warnings — build54, 30 October 2026

Problem: per-coin price revalidation used only accepted candlestick_5m messages. No independent ticker subscription or recent-warning quote fallback existed. A coin could therefore lose its fresh-price status while another coin kept the overall feed badge live. That did not establish that the original range break had failed. A user's local transport cannot be retrospectively diagnosed from screenshots alone.

Fix: subscribe to Delta's public ticker channel alongside candlestick_5m on the existing socket. Parse native compact ticker rows d[].s and ohlc[3] (traded-price close), paired with native packet ts. Never substitute mark/index/mid prices for this rule. Ticker observations update current price, source age and observed range re-entry independently of candle volume validation. Separate candle and quote source ordering prevents a newer ticker from blocking candle analysis, or an older candle from overwriting a newer price. Ticker messages alone never create volume alerts.

For non-invalidated warnings at most10minutes old whose own price is older than10seconds, a separate bounded fallback attempts at most2 public symbol-ticker reads per tick and no more than once per10seconds per symbol. It is independent of baseline download busy state, serialised by a separate guard, and checks exact returned symbol, valid positive price, provider source age≤15seconds, future tolerance and monotonic ordering. A fetch arrival time does not renew the quote timestamp. Pause/generation changes discard late replies. Socket/market-coverage health requirements remain; a healthy unrelated coin never substitutes for the warning coin. If both paths fail, it remains unverified/stale. No guarantees of complete feed availability or zero latency.

Current source provenance (Ticker stream / REST ticker / Candle stream) appears beside per-coin source age. The original warning time, volume, direction and range level remain recorded. Forming warnings remain early activity detections, not confirmed entries. Data stale means no usable recent observation; invalidated means observed price range re-entry; expired means the original warning's10minute review window ended. None automatically closes/changes a real position or instructs an exit. User copy now explicitly distinguishes stale/expired from an exit signal. Existing broker trade monitoring and its acceptance limits remain separate.

Protocol verification: official https://docs.delta.exchange/ documents the public ticker channel, compact payload and5second publish cadence. A read-only public BTCUSD probe received its subscription acknowledgment and real native ticker payload. This is not all250symbols / customer-machine acceptance. New cases verify ticker price continuity without a volume candle, no ticker-created signal, ticker-observed invalidation, independent out-of-order candle/quote handling, rejection of malformed/stale ticker data, exact-symbol and source-time fallback validation, and paused late-response discard. Existing broker/owner/localhost tests pass; no real orders or messages were sent.

No keys, recipients, positions, leverage, margin, risk limits, sharing or strategy thresholds changed. Local terminal/awake-PC and hosted open-browser limitations remain; no always-running hosted collector was provisioned.
