# Early Rally Radar — build 47, 29 October 2026

## What is built
Early Rally Radar replaces the existing Unusual activity setup tab, keeping its saved enable/pause controls and Top20 universe. It combines automatic price/volume, multi-timeframe breakouts, a separate closed retest, sampled OI/funding, public liquidation context and persisted source-backed fundamental evidence. It is a screening tool, with no return probability, order placement or automatic risk changes.

Fundamental evidence is OWNER-REVIEWED, not automatically researched or independently verified. This version does not connect a licensed news, unlock or audit-verification feed. Saving a source URL does not prove the claim. This distinction appears in the page status, editor and stored provenance. Missing or expired reviews cannot qualify. Do not present this version as autonomous fundamental due diligence or a validated multibagger predictor.

## Workflow
Crypto → Setups → Early Rally Radar → Start continuous scan. Select Delta India for supported derivatives context. The scanner retains the existing Top20 turnover scope. Review a candidate's sources, or use Review a scanned coin when no candidates are present. Supply exact project/network identity, a factual catalyst and date, source links for catalyst/unlocks/security/holder concentration, and your reviewed outcomes. Defaults remain unknown. An explicit identity/source review attestation is required for qualification. A review is fresh for 24 hours. Catalyst date must be within the preceding 30 days or following 7 days to qualify. Source links are stored as evidence and never fetched by this endpoint; private/credential-bearing URLs are rejected.

Automatic technical requirements for QUALIFIED: fresh observed market check no older than six minutes, with a source quote within 15 seconds when that check was made; upside activity, latest closed 5m volume ≥3× baseline, at least two aligned breakout frames; a breakout out of a preceding 20×5m range no wider than 10%, followed by a separate closed retest within the latest six bars, no subsequent closes back inside, live price above the level; 24h turnover ≥$1M and bid/ask spread ≤0.3%; sampled contract OI growth ≥3%; funding baseline available and not extreme; liquidation context available and no spike. These are explicit heuristics, not calibrated probabilities. Requiring all of them can correctly produce no qualified rows.

Watch = current candidate with a complete clear fundamental review but unfinished technical gates. Unverified = missing/expired evidence, stale market checks, missing required data or failed scanner. Avoid = current funding crowding, a liquidation spike or a fresh source-backed owner concern about unlocks/security/concentration. Paused and No signal are explicit internal states; the main list shows upside activity candidates only. Coverage is never used as a confidence percentage. Invalidation is a chart reference, not an active exchange stop.

Candidate cards show three reasons, missing/waiting checks, invalidation reference, individual IST check time and a TradingView chart link. Source details stay collapsible. The existing feed diagnostics and raw technical history are under collapsed sections. Saving a review re-evaluates the saved market snapshot; it does not fetch a fresh quote. Stale snapshots remain unverified until the next real scan.

## Persistence and alerts
Owner-scoped review records use existing database storage and optimistic revision guards. Concurrent/stale saves are rejected instead of overwriting newer reviews. Editable forms survive refreshes; navigation to another review is blocked until save or explicit close. Writes require owner identity and same-origin JSON. No migrations/keys/recipients or sharing changes.

Qualified-review events are stored, deduplicated for one hour per symbol, bounded in history, and displayed as bold title-only in-app alerts when still supported by the current qualified row and review revision. No new early WhatsApp delivery path is introduced. Existing hourly confirmation messages retain their saved settings. Historical detections are labelled and do not establish a current setup.

Local terminal runner continues enabled scans with browser closed; initial sampled baselines take time. The hosted-only app still has no persistent liquidation collector; it will show unavailable context and cannot satisfy the complete qualification gate from that context alone. Shark derivatives context remains unsupported. Global markets and Indian order execution limitations remain in UAT-REPORT.md.
