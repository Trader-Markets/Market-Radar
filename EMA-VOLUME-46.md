# 200 EMA + volume scanner — build 46

Location: Crypto → Setups → Market scanner → 5 · 200 EMA + volume. No additional workspace or setup tab was added.

Rule: use completed 4H candles, at least 221 contiguous valid bars (the request loads about 330). Seed the 200 EMA with a 200-close simple mean, then update recursively using 2/201. Previous candle close must be at or below its corresponding EMA; the latest completed candle close must be above its corresponding EMA. Latest closed volume must be at least twice the preceding 20 closed bars' mean, excluding itself. Zero baseline is unavailable. An already-above-EMA coin does not qualify as a new cross. A forming candle cannot trigger it. A fresh quote, no older than 15 seconds and not in the future, must still be above the computed EMA when the row is accepted.

The scan uses the selected Delta/Shark market and existing minimum-turnover filter; it is not restricted to the Top20 feature. It is a browser-run scan, with explicit Scan coins and Stop scan. Selecting it cancels the regular four-strategy scan and prevents that auto-scan from competing while this strategy is selected. Existing strategy buttons restore their original view. Market changes discard old results; stop and replacement generations ignore late responses. Successful checks, matches and unavailable checks are counted separately. A no-match result is not an error.

Rows show coin, closed price, EMA, volume multiple, candle close and individual check time in IST. Chart opens an inline full TradingView chart for the selected market when supported; a direct TradingView link is the fallback. Shark uses an explicitly labelled Binance reference chart, while scanner calculations use Shark candles. The chart provider controls its tools and data; the scanner does not claim automatic EMA drawing. No orders or new alert deliveries are created by this scanner. It does not assess token safety, fundamentals or future return probability.
